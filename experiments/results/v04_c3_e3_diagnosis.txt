Result in one line: C3 and E3 fail for different reasons, and neither reason is K5.

  * E3: class 8 (other), concretely an evaluation defect plus a coverage effect. The v0.2 pass

C3 hidden condition - class 6, replay/transfer evidence accounting (15/15 seeds)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The decisive law is not a refinement child in either learner. In v0.2 the conditional law that
answers the USE queries is a single-feature law, "rK.c3=1 => door changes", where rK is the
power role and c3 the ON channel. It is created on its first case and licensed with transfer
13/13. The phase-1 parent (unconditional law) has 0 children in both learners, so classes 1, 2,
4 and 5 do not apply.

In v0.3 the same hypothesis is deferred during phase 1. Power is constant there, so its
information against the base model is ~0. It becomes informative in phase 2 and is materialized
by replay (D055) only after many cases. Replay gives counts and keys but no transfer credit
(D014). It therefore marks every relevant situation already seen as seen. The door world has
only 16 relevant situations for this law and all were seen before materialization, so no later
case is ever novel. Transfer stays 0/0 forever and the law can never be licensed. Its utility
is above threshold in every seed, and counterexamples are 0. Classes 3 and 6 coincide here:
the law is materialized but never licensed because of transfer accounting.

The negative-side law, "rK.c3=0 => no change", is licensed in both learners with transfer
13/13. It is materialized earlier, while unseen situations remained.

Consequence. Half of the USE queries (power on) abstain in v0.3. This matches the v0.3 record
of ~100 correct and ~100 abstain per seed.

Why this is not K5. The licence cost is not the binding constraint: utility (66-95 bits)
exceeds log2(n_cand) + margin in both learners. The missing ingredient is held-out evidence,
and in a closed world that evidence can only be gathered prospectively, while the hypothesis is
still deferred.

Repair direction (to be pre-registered as a dated amendment before implementation). A deferred
value keeps the same prospective transfer record a law would keep. When a case arrives:
  * if the value already predicts (>= GATE_MIN_CASES cases, majority within noise, >= 2 relevant
    bindings), and
  * the case's relevant situation is provably unseen for that value,
then the value's current majority is recorded as a pre-registered prediction before the outcome
is counted. On materialization the law inherits these tallies.

This is not retroactive credit: every trial was predicted before its outcome was read, by a
hypothesis that existed (deferred) at that time. Memory: one bounded relevant-situation key set
plus two counters per deferred entry. The bound and its effect on H5 / C4b memory are measured
in development.

Findings.

(a) The v0.2 pass is produced by quantity, not by choice.
  * v0.2 sleep creates ~8,000 laws per seed, ~1,500 of them unlicensed.
  * Every one of the five options (USE key, TOGGLE x3, WAIT) matches 150-250 of them.
  * In v0.2 the chosen action at step 0 is WAIT on the inspected seeds (46, 58). WAIT has the
    lowest total score: no residual ambiguity from licensed laws, small information gain. It
    counts as "testing sleep hypotheses" because ~200 unlicensed sleep laws are about WAIT.
  * Any policy would have scored ~30/30 on this measure.

(b) The twin comparison is defective in both builds.
  * The twin engine is a replay of the store without sleep (clone_engine; sleep is never run on
    it).
  * It tests membership in sleep_laws, a set of law INDICES from the other engine.
  * Its counts are index coincidences: 0 in v0.2, where the twin has fewer laws than the first
    sleep index; 0-19 in v0.3.
  * The ">= 2x twin" half of the criterion therefore measured nothing in the v0.2 regression of
    record.

(c) v0.3 coverage.
  * The D055 refinement gate keeps sleep to 49-142 new laws, 39-114 of them unlicensed.
  * In 13/15 seeds none of the five options matches any of them at step 0. In seeds 55 and 58
    one option matches 16 / 32, but the policy prefers WAIT (total 0.228 vs 0.251; 0.225 vs
    0.390).
  * Sleep refinement does generate children: 192 of 285 sleep refinement attempts on seed 46
    passed the gate. Those that pass are mostly licensed or revoked within sleep by the held-out
    split (D048), so little is left for wake to verify.

Why this is not K5. The E3 result depends on how many unlicensed sleep hypotheses exist and
which actions they are about, not on the licence cost.

Consequence for PREREG-v0.4. The pre-registration requires every gate that passed in the v0.2
regression of record to pass. Finding (b) means the E3 gate as recorded cannot discriminate.
Finding (a) means the v0.2 pass would be reproduced by restoring hypothesis over-generation, not
by better verification. Restoring over-generation to pass E3 would reverse the memory result and
is not done.

The proposed treatment is a dated amendment made before any v0.4 learner change:
  * correct the twin (it runs the same sleep, and its own sleep-created laws are used);
  * re-measure the v0.2 baseline and the candidate with the corrected harness;
  * E3 counts as a must-pass gate only if v0.2 passes it under the corrected measurement.

This is a change to a pre-registered criterion and is reported as such in the final report.


Classes not observed: 1 (parent never examined), 2 (parent deferred: the C3 parent is

Addendum (same day): E3 re-measured with the corrected twin
-----------------------------------------------------------
The harness was corrected as proposed: the twin replays the same store, runs the same sleep, and
tests its own sleep-created laws. Both learners were re-run on seeds 46-60, with no learner
change.

  * v0.2 learner: FAIL on 15/15 seeds; active 30, fixed-rotation twin 30 on every seed.
    The recorded v0.2 pass was produced by the defect: any action tests some of the ~1,500
    unlicensed sleep hypotheses.
  * v0.3 learner: FAIL on 15/15 seeds; active 0, twin 0-9 (seeds 46: 2, 52: 4, 55: 9, 58: 9,
    others 0).
    The active policy prefers WAIT even where a fixed rotation would test sleep hypotheses. This
    is a real, if small, weakness of the D043 scoring when few hypotheses exist. It is reported,
    not repaired, because E3 is out of the must-pass set; see PREREG-v0.4 amendment 1.

Under a valid measurement E3 is therefore a v0.2 baseline failure, like E1 seed 54 and H2 seeds
210-211.
